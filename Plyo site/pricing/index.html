What you don't pay

No commissions. No kickbacks.

Plyo is not a marketplace. We don't take a cut of the factory's invoice and we don't accept kickbacks from suppliers. The factory quotes you their ex-factory price. You pay them directly. We charge a flat fee on top.

Why this matters: every agent paid by the factory has an incentive to keep prices high. Ours is the opposite — we're paid to find you the lowest verified ex-factory price.

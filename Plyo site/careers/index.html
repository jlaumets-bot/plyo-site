Apply as an Agent

Stop hunting for clients. Start executing orders.

Plyo is a procurement infrastructure layer. We bring serious Western importers to the table. You bring the factory access. We handle the marketing, contracts, and legal layer. You handle execution.

The Plyo standard

We don't hire generalists.

If you say "I can source anything," this network isn't for you. We only accept agents with deep roots in specific manufacturing sectors. Five non-negotiable baseline criteria:

Deep niche expertise

You specialise in 1–2 specific industries (e.g. consumer electronics, textiles, heavy machinery, furniture). You know the factory hubs for your niche by name.

Boots on the ground

You are physically able and willing to travel to factories for live, GPS-tracked video audits and quality control inspections.

Zero-kickback policy

You operate with 100% transparency. Your income comes from our agent fees. Hidden kickbacks or secret commissions from factories result in immediate termination and blacklisting.

Professional English

Strong written and spoken English. You communicate directly with Western buyers inside the WhatsApp platform.

Established network

You have a pre-existing network of reliable, high-quality manufacturers — not just Alibaba links.

3-Step verification

Earning the Plyo Verified badge.

We protect our clients' capital relentlessly. Joining the network requires passing a strict vetting process.

01

Identity & business verification

You provide your official Chinese National ID and/or your company's Business Licence (营业执照). We run background checks to verify you are exactly who you say you are.

02

Track record audit

You provide proof of past success. Sanitised examples of previous sourcing projects — product spec, factory used, negotiated price, final QC report.

03

Live test-order interview

If you pass the paper checks, you'll have a video interview with our European management team. We give you a mock spec and evaluate how you'd source it, negotiate it, and protect the client from common defects.

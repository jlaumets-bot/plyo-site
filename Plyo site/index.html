Who we serve

Anyone importing from China who has been burned — or is trying to avoid being burned.

Whether you're placing your first €10k order or your fiftieth €200k production run, the failure modes are the same: middlemen disguised as factories, samples that don't match production, contracts that aren't enforceable, and no way to verify what's actually happening 8,000 km away.

Plyo replaces all of it with one layer: a verified agent, a legal contract, a live audit feed.
